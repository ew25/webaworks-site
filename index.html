What we do

Applied AI and data science, end to end

We work two ways: as consultants on AI systems for clients, and as the team that owns our own products end to end. Both draw on the same capability.

  • 🧠 Applied AI & LLM systems

    We design and ship AI systems against real cost, quality, and privacy constraints — not demos. Pilots are scoped and described as pilots; production systems are built to run unattended.

  • 📊 Experimentation & analytics

    Instrumentation, experiment design, and the analytics layer behind a product decision — the same infrastructure we rely on to run ExamenPatron and Suma ourselves.

  • 🗄️ Data & decision infrastructure

    The data pipelines, storage, and decision logic that sit behind a product or an internal tool — owned end to end, on EU-based infrastructure, with privacy and data protection built in by design.
